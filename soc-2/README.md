# SOC 2 Penetration Testing Checklist

Prepare an evidence-ready penetration-testing program that can support SOC 2 scoping, risk management, and remediation tracking.

**[Download the SOC 2 Penetration Testing Checklist](./SOC-2-Penetration-Testing-Checklist.pdf)**

## Who should use it

- SaaS and technology organizations preparing for a SOC 2 examination
- Security and engineering teams responsible for application, API, cloud, and external-attack-surface security
- vCISOs and compliance advisers coordinating testing evidence

## What it covers

- Scope definition and written authorization
- External network and cloud infrastructure review
- Web application, API, authentication, and authorization testing
- Data protection and secrets-exposure validation
- Detection, response, remediation, and retest evidence

## Preparing useful SOC 2 evidence

Keep the approved scope, methodology, tester qualifications, dated findings report, remediation records, and retest results together. Align the evidence package to the criteria and control activities selected by your organization and service auditor.

SOC 2 reports are examinations of relevant controls against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria. This checklist helps teams organize testing evidence; it does not determine the outcome of a SOC 2 examination.
