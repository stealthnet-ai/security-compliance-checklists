# CMMC Penetration Testing Checklist

Prepare an authorized penetration-testing program for systems that process, store, or transmit Federal Contract Information or Controlled Unclassified Information.

**[Download the CMMC Penetration Testing Checklist](./CMMC-Penetration-Testing-Checklist.pdf)**

## Who should use it

- Defense Industrial Base contractors and subcontractors
- Information-system security managers and facility-security teams
- CMMC advisers and authorized security-assessment partners

## What it covers

- CUI boundary and system-security-plan alignment
- Network and enclave segmentation
- Identity, least privilege, remote access, and credential controls
- Endpoint, configuration, data-protection, audit, and incident-response testing
- Evidence, remediation, and retest planning

## Preparing useful assessment evidence

Retain the approved rules of engagement, scope, system boundary diagrams, methodology, dated findings, remediation records, and validation evidence. Align the assessment approach to applicable contract requirements and the current CMMC, DFARS, and NIST requirements.

CMMC program status and assessment requirements can change. Confirm the applicable contract clause, current program status, and assessment guidance before relying on a checklist for an assessment decision.
